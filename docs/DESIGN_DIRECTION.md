# Apex4Traders interface direction

The product takes structural inspiration from Kraken Pro's widget-based trading
workspace and from the Saved references `Spendable Banking App` and `Clean Tab
Switching Animation`. It does not copy their branding, artwork, or content.

## Product decisions

- Lead with the selected account, automation state, and risk state.
- Keep the market chart, rule context, positions, and journal in one workspace.
- Use compact cards with clear hierarchy instead of decorative dashboards.
- Use animated tab indicators to show state changes without moving the layout.
- Use real broker data only. Missing data is shown as `—` with a reason.
- Keep the same information hierarchy on mobile, changing layout rather than
  shrinking desktop tables.

## Visual language

The dashboard uses a graphite terminal surface with restrained blue interaction
accents and semantic green/red states. Public and account pages retain the
lighter editorial surface so the terminal remains a purposeful work mode.

Images belong to the marketing site and product tour. They must be labelled as
product previews when they do not come from a connected broker. No image may
imply a return, win rate, balance, or live execution that the product has not
verified.

## Component priorities

1. Account and execution status ribbon.
2. Chart and market context.
3. Rule preview and approval state.
4. Risk guard and emergency controls.
5. Positions, orders, and journal tabs.
6. Responsive mobile navigation and detail views.
