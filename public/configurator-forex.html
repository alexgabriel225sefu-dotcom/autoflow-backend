MT5 Bridge setup — one-time, ~15 minutes:

1

Deploy the bot on Render with your OANDA or placeholder credentials first.

2

On your Windows PC or VPS, install MetaTrader 5 and log in to your MT5 broker account.

3

In MT5, open Tools → Options → Expert Advisors. Enable "Allow WebRequest" and add your Render URL.

4

Copy ApexBridge.mq5 (included in the source code) to your MT5 MQL5/Experts folder and compile it.

5

Attach the EA to a chart of your instrument. Set ServerURL = your Render URL, SecretKey = your DASHBOARD_TOKEN.

6

The EA syncs every 10s. Signals generated by the bot are executed by the EA on your MT5 account.

📄 Paper TradingSimulated positions. No real funds moved. Recommended first 1–2 weeks.
🔴 Live TradingReal positions on your broker account. Activate only after paper validation.
⚠ Live trading uses real funds. Forex losses can exceed your deposit due to leverage. Start with a small account.
