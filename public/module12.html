Strategy · Lesson 1

The Proposal System That Closes More Deals

Most freelancers lose deals because their proposals are either too slow (sent days after the call) or too generic (clearly a template with the name changed). This system sends a personalized, professional proposal within 2 hours of every call — automatically.

Why Fast Proposals Win
Research on B2B sales shows that proposals sent within 2 hours of a discovery call close at 3x the rate of proposals sent 24+ hours later. The client is most excited immediately after the call. A fast, professional proposal capitalizes on that excitement before it fades — and before they talk to a competitor.
The problem: writing a good proposal manually takes 1–3 hours. By the time most agency owners finish writing it, the momentum is gone. The AI Proposal Generator solves this — you fill in a simple form immediately after the call and the full proposal is ready in 60 seconds.
What the Perfect Proposal Includes
1
Executive Summary — their problem in their words
Start with a 2–3 sentence summary of their specific situation. Reference what they told you on the call — the exact pain points they described. This immediately shows you listened and understood. It makes the rest of the proposal feel tailored, not generic.
2
Proposed Solution — exactly what you will build
Describe the automation system in plain language — not technical jargon. Focus on what it does for their business, not how it works technically. Include: what triggers it, what it does automatically, what the outcome is for their customers.
3
Expected Results — specific and measurable
Include 3–5 specific expected results. For example: "Every customer inquiry receives a reply within 60 seconds instead of 4–6 hours." "Your team saves approximately 8 hours per week on manual message replies." These concrete numbers justify the price.
4
Investment — clear pricing with justification
Present the setup fee and monthly retainer clearly. Add one sentence justifying the price: "Most clients recover this investment within the first month through leads that were previously lost to slow response times."
5
Next Steps — clear call to action
End with exactly what the client needs to do to proceed. "Reply to this email with 'Yes, let's start' and I will send the invoice for the 50% deposit. We can begin immediately after payment is received."
✦ The Proposal Mindset
A proposal is not a document — it is a sales tool. Every sentence should move the client closer to saying yes. The Executive Summary shows you listened. The Solution section shows you can solve their problem. The Results section justifies the price. The Next Steps make it easy to say yes. Write every section with this goal in mind.
Key Takeaways — Lesson 1
Proposals sent within 2 hours close at 3x the rate of those sent 24+ hours later.
Every proposal section has a sales purpose — listening proof, solution clarity, ROI justification, easy next step.
Reference specific things the client said on the call — it makes the proposal feel tailored, not templated.
Always include concrete expected results with numbers — hours saved, response time reduced, leads not lost.
Build · Lesson 2

Build the AI Proposal Generator in Make.com

You fill in a Google Form immediately after a sales call with the client's details. Make.com picks it up, sends everything to GPT-4o, and a complete professional proposal is generated and emailed to the client within 60 seconds.

Step 1 — Create the Post-Call Google Form
1
Create a new Google Form called "Post-Call Notes"
Go to forms.google.com. Create a new form. This form is private — only you fill it in, right after every sales call. Name it "Post-Call Notes — Proposal Generator."
2
Add these fields to the form
Client Name: Short answer — the business owner's name
Business Name: Short answer — the company name
Client Email: Short answer with email validation
Industry: Dropdown — Restaurant, Real Estate, Beauty, Clinic, E-commerce, Other
Automation Type: Dropdown — WhatsApp Bot, Instagram DM, Lead Follow-Up, Voice Agent, Full Package
Main Pain Point (their words): Paragraph — write exactly what they said their problem is
Setup Price: Short answer — e.g. $800
Monthly Retainer: Short answer — e.g. $250
Notes from the call: Paragraph — anything specific they mentioned, objections, timeline preferences
3
Link the form to Google Sheets
Click the Responses tab → click the green Sheets icon → click Create. A Google Sheet is created automatically. This is what Make.com will watch.
Step 2 — Build the Make.com Scenario
1
Create a new Scenario with Google Sheets trigger
In Make.com, create a new Scenario. Add Google Sheets → Watch New Rows. Connect to your Post-Call Notes spreadsheet. Set Limit to 1. Click OK.
2
Add OpenAI to generate the full proposal
Add OpenAI → Create a Chat Completion. Model: gpt-4o. In the Message content field paste this prompt, inserting variables from Google Sheets:
Write a professional AI automation agency proposal email for this client. Client Information: - Client Name: {{1.Client_Name}} - Business: {{1.Business_Name}} - Industry: {{1.Industry}} - Automation needed: {{1.Automation_Type}} - Their main problem: {{1.Main_Pain_Point}} - Setup investment: {{1.Setup_Price}} - Monthly retainer: {{1.Monthly_Retainer}} - Call notes: {{1.Notes_from_the_call}} Write a complete proposal email with these sections: 1. Subject line (write "SUBJECT:" before it) 2. Greeting 3. Executive Summary (2 sentences referencing their specific situation) 4. Proposed Solution (what you will build, in plain language, 3-4 sentences) 5. Expected Results (3 specific bullet points with numbers) 6. Investment section (setup fee and monthly retainer with one justification sentence) 7. Clear Next Steps 8. Professional closing Tone: professional, confident, warm. NOT corporate or robotic. Length: 300-400 words total. Do NOT use placeholders like [Company Name] — use the real information provided.
3
Parse the subject line from the GPT output
Add a Text Parser module. This separates the subject line from the email body. In Make.com, search for Text Parser → select Match Pattern.
Pattern: SUBJECT: (.+)\n
Text: the OpenAI output variable

This extracts everything after "SUBJECT: " on the first line as your email subject. The rest of the GPT output becomes the email body.
4
Send the proposal via Gmail
Add Gmail → Send an Email.
To: variable from Google Sheets → Client Email
Subject: the parsed subject line from Text Parser
Content: OpenAI output variable (full proposal text)

Click OK. Turn on Scheduling. Save the Scenario.
5
Test the complete flow
Fill in the Post-Call Notes form with test data — use your own email as the client email. Click Run once in Make.com. Check your inbox — the proposal email should arrive within 30 seconds. Read it and verify it sounds professional and personalized.
✓ Result
After every sales call, you spend 3 minutes filling in the form on your phone. Within 60 seconds the client receives a professional, personalized proposal in their inbox. You are the most responsive, professional agency they have ever encountered — and you did it in 3 minutes total work.
Key Takeaways — Lesson 2
Fill in the Post-Call form immediately after the call — while the details are fresh. Never delay more than 10 minutes.
The more detail you put in "Main Pain Point" and "Notes from the call" fields, the better the proposal GPT generates.
The Text Parser extracts the subject line — this is critical for the email to have the right subject, not the full proposal as subject.
Test with your own email first before it ever goes to a real client.
Sales · Lesson 3

Track Proposals and Follow Up Automatically

Sending the proposal is only half the battle. Most deals are won on the follow-up. This lesson adds automatic follow-up emails 3 days and 7 days after the proposal — without you remembering to do anything.

Step 1 — Add Proposal Tracking to Your CRM Sheet
1
Add a "Proposals" tab to your Google Sheets CRM
Open your main CRM Google Sheet from Module 8. At the bottom, click the + to add a new tab. Name it "Proposals." Set up these columns:
Column A: Timestamp (when proposal was sent)
Column B: Client Name
Column C: Client Email
Column D: Business Name
Column E: Proposal Amount (setup price)
Column F: Status — New/Followed Up/Won/Lost
Column G: Follow Up 1 Sent (date)
Column H: Follow Up 2 Sent (date)
Column I: Notes
2
Update the proposal scenario to log each proposal
In your Make.com proposal scenario, after the Gmail module, add Google Sheets → Add a Row. Connect to the Proposals tab. Map: Timestamp = now, Client Name, Client Email, Business Name, Setup Price from the form fields. Status = "New". Click OK.
Step 2 — Automatic Follow-Up at Day 3
1
Create a new Make.com Scenario for follow-ups
Create a new Scenario. Set the trigger to Schedule → Every Day at 9:00 AM.
2
Search for proposals that need follow-up
Add Google Sheets → Search Rows. Connect to the Proposals tab. Add filters:
Filter 1: Column F (Status) = "New"
Filter 2: Column A (Timestamp) is between 3 and 4 days ago

This finds all proposals sent 3 days ago that have not been responded to yet.
3
Generate and send the follow-up email
Add OpenAI → Create a Chat Completion. Prompt:
Write a short, friendly follow-up email for a proposal sent 3 days ago. Client name: {{client_name}} Business: {{business_name}} What they were interested in: {{automation_type}} Proposal amount: {{amount}} The email should: - Be maximum 80 words - Reference the proposal sent a few days ago - Create gentle urgency (limited availability this month) - End with a simple yes/no question - Sound like a real person, not a sales robot
After OpenAI, add Gmail → Send an Email to the client email. Subject: "Re: Your automation proposal — [Business Name]". Content: OpenAI output.
4
Mark the row as "Followed Up"
After sending the email, add Google Sheets → Update a Row. Set Column F (Status) to "Followed Up" and Column G (Follow Up 1 Sent) to today's date. This prevents the same client from getting another Day 3 follow-up tomorrow.
Step 3 — Day 7 Final Follow-Up
Create an identical scenario but filter for Status = "Followed Up" AND Timestamp between 7 and 8 days ago. The Day 7 email is shorter and more direct — either they are interested or they are not, and you want to find out.
Write a final 50-word follow-up email for a proposal sent 7 days ago with no response. Client: {{client_name}} The email should ask directly if they are still interested or if timing is not right. No pressure. Sound genuine and human. If they say no, offer to reconnect in 3 months.
✦ The Revenue Impact
Most agency owners follow up on 30% of proposals — they forget or feel awkward. With this system, 100% of proposals get followed up automatically, on time, every time. Industry data shows that 50% of sales happen after the 5th follow-up. Most people stop after 1. This automated follow-up system alone can double your closing rate.
Key Takeaways — Lesson 3
Automated follow-ups run 100% consistently — unlike manual follow-ups which get forgotten.
Always update the Status column after each follow-up — prevents duplicate emails.
Day 3 follow-up: create urgency. Day 7 follow-up: ask directly if they are still interested.
The complete proposal system (generate + send + follow up × 2) takes 3 minutes of your time per client — everything else is automated.
