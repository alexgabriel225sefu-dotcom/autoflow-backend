Prospecting · Lesson 1

Apollo.io — Find 100 Verified Prospects in 10 Minutes

Apollo.io is a B2B database with 275+ million verified contacts. You search by job title, industry, company size, and location — and get real, verified email addresses of business owners and decision makers. The free plan gives you 50 email exports per month. Follow every step below click by click.

What Apollo.io Is and Why It's the Best Free Tool
Most prospecting tools show you partial information — a name but no email, or an email but no job title. Apollo shows you everything: the CEO's direct email, their LinkedIn URL, company revenue estimate, headcount, and even what software the company uses. This lets you target exactly who you want and personalize your outreach with real data.
The free plan is genuinely useful. 50 verified email exports per month is enough to run multiple campaigns while you are getting your first clients. No credit card is required to sign up.
Step 1 — Create Your Free Apollo.io Account
1
Go to apollo.io in your browser
Open a new browser tab. In the address bar type apollo.io and press Enter. The Apollo homepage loads — you see a headline about finding prospects and a large green button. Do not sign in with LinkedIn first — use Google instead for the fastest setup.
2
Click "Get started for free" and sign up with Google
Click the large Get started for free button. The signup page opens. Click Sign up with Google — the blue button with a Google icon. A Google account selector popup appears. Click your Google account. Apollo creates your account automatically. No password needed, no credit card needed. You are logged in.
You land on the Apollo dashboard. The interface has a dark left sidebar with navigation items and a main content area. Take 30 seconds to look around — you see "Search" at the top, "Sequences" for email automation, and "Contacts" for saved prospects.
3
Skip the LinkedIn connection for now
Apollo may show a prompt to connect your LinkedIn account. Click Skip or Maybe later — you can add LinkedIn later. You can use Apollo fully without it. The contact data is slightly more detailed with LinkedIn connected but it works either way.
Step 2 — Search for Your First 100 Prospects
1
Click "Search" in the top navigation bar
In the left sidebar of the Apollo dashboard, find and click Search. A dropdown menu appears showing two options: People and Companies. Click People. The People search page loads — you see a results panel on the right and a filter panel on the left.
The results show all 275 million contacts before any filters are applied. Do not be overwhelmed — the filters in the next steps narrow this down to exactly the people you want.
2
Set the Job Title filter — who you want to reach
In the left filter panel, find Job Title. Click on it — a text input field expands below it. Type the first job title and press Enter. Then type the next one and press Enter. Add as many as are relevant to your target:
For restaurants and food businesses: Owner, Restaurant Owner, Manager
For beauty and wellness: Owner, Founder, Studio Owner
For e-commerce: CEO, Founder, E-commerce Manager
For general small business: Owner, CEO, Founder, Managing Director

After each job title you type, Apollo shows a suggestion list. Click the matching suggestion to confirm the selection. A tag appears showing the selected title with an X to remove it.
3
Set the Industry filter — narrow to your niche
In the filter panel, find Industry. Click on it. Type your target industry in the text field. Apollo shows matching industry categories — click the one that matches. Examples to type:
Restaurants → select "Food & Beverages" or "Restaurants"
Fitness → select "Health, Wellness and Fitness"
Beauty → select "Cosmetics" or "Health, Wellness and Fitness"
Real estate → select "Real Estate"
Retail → select "Retail"

You can select multiple industries by clicking each one — they appear as tags.
4
Set the Location filter — your target city or country
Find Location in the filter panel. Click it. Type your target location — start with a city name or country. Apollo shows location suggestions — click the right one. Examples:
Bucharest, Romania → selects the city
Romania → selects the entire country
United Kingdom → selects the UK
New York, United States → selects New York city

You can add multiple locations by clicking each one. Start narrow (one city) and broaden if you don't get enough results.
5
Set Company Size to 1–50 employees
Find Company Headcount or # Employees in the filter panel. Click it. Check the boxes for 1–10 and 11–50. These are small businesses where the owner is usually the decision maker and can say yes on a call the same day.
Why avoid large companies for your first clients? Companies with 200+ employees have procurement departments, multiple approval layers, and 3–6 month sales cycles. Small businesses (1–50 employees) often pay on the same call if they see the value. Start small, get case studies, then target larger companies later.
6
Check the results count and adjust if needed
After setting all filters, look at the top of the results panel. Apollo shows the total number of matching contacts — for example "1,247 people found." You want between 200 and 3,000 results for a good campaign.
If under 100 results: broaden your filters — add more job titles, expand the location to a region instead of one city, or remove the company size filter.

If over 10,000 results: narrow further — add a more specific industry, reduce location to one city, or add a "Technologies Used" filter (search for companies using Shopify or WordPress — these are tech-comfortable businesses).
7
Select contacts and export to CSV
At the top of the results list, check the Select All checkbox — this selects all 25 contacts on the current page. Repeat for the next 3 pages (total 100 contacts). Then find the Export button — it shows a download arrow icon, usually at the top right of the results. Click it. Select Export to CSV.
Apollo uses your free export credits. The free plan allows 50 email exports per month. If you need more: create a second free account with a different Google account (each gets 50 credits), or upgrade to the Basic plan ($49/month, 300 exports).

The CSV downloads to your computer's Downloads folder. Open it to verify — you should see columns: First Name, Last Name, Email, Company, Title, City, LinkedIn URL, and more. The emails should be real business addresses, not info@ addresses.
8
Open the CSV and copy it to Google Sheets
Open the downloaded CSV in Excel or by opening Google Sheets and importing it. Go to sheets.google.com → click + Blank → click File → Import → Upload → drag your CSV file in → click Import data. Save the sheet as "Prospects — [Industry] — [Month Year]." You will upload this to Instantly.ai in Lesson 2.
✦ The Technology Filter — Pre-Qualify Your Prospects
In Apollo's filter panel, find Technologies Used. Search for Shopify or HubSpot. Companies using these tools already understand software tools that save time — they are pre-qualified buyers for your automation services. Shopify users are also e-commerce businesses with high customer inquiry volume, making them perfect Instagram and WhatsApp bot clients.
Key Takeaways — Lesson 1
Apollo.io free plan: 50 email exports per month, no credit card, sign up with Google in under 2 minutes.
Always filter by Company Size 1–50 employees for your first clients — they decide and pay faster.
Aim for 200–3,000 results before exporting — too few means your niche is too narrow, too many means you need more filters.
Save the CSV to Google Sheets and name it clearly — you will use it in Lesson 2 and Lesson 3.
✦ Do This Right Now
Open apollo.io and create your account right now. Do not plan to do it later — do it now. Set up the filters for the industry and location you chose. Aim for 300–1,000 results. Export the first 100 contacts to CSV. Open the CSV and count how many have real email addresses (not "email not found"). You need at least 80 real emails from your first 100 contacts. If you have fewer, your industry or location filters may be too narrow — broaden them and export again.
Email Sending · Lesson 2

Instantly.ai — Send 50 Personalized Emails Per Day Automatically

Instantly.ai is a cold email platform that warms up your sending address, manages daily limits to avoid spam filters, and sends your sequence automatically. Without a tool like Instantly, Gmail will suspend your account within days of sending cold emails in volume. Follow every step exactly.

Why You Cannot Use Plain Gmail for Cold Email
When you send 50 identical cold emails from a regular Gmail account, Google detects the pattern within 2–3 days and either sends your emails to the spam folder or suspends the account entirely. Instantly.ai solves this in two ways: email warmup (gradually builds your sender reputation so email providers trust your address) and smart sending (spaces out emails to look like a real human sending them one by one).
Step 1 — Create Your Instantly.ai Account
1
Go to instantly.ai and start the free trial
Open a new tab and go to instantly.ai. Click the Start Free Trial button. A registration form appears. Enter your name, email address, and create a password. Click Create Account. Check your email inbox for a verification email and click the confirmation link. You are now in the Instantly.ai dashboard.
Instantly.ai offers a 14-day free trial of their Growth plan ($37/month after trial). The Growth plan allows up to 5,000 emails per month across unlimited sending accounts. Start the trial — you have 14 days to test it and cancel if you decide not to continue.
2
Click "Add Account" and connect your sending email
In the Instantly.ai dashboard left sidebar, click Email Accounts. Click + Add Account. Two connection options appear. Click Google / Gmail. A Google account selector popup appears — click the email account you want to use for sending. On the permissions screen, click Allow. The email account is now connected — it appears in your accounts list.
Best practice: Use a custom domain email like yourname@youragency.com instead of a plain Gmail address. Custom domain emails have significantly better deliverability for cold outreach. You can get a domain at Namecheap for ~$10/year and add Google Workspace for $6/month. The $16/month investment increases your reply rate by 30–50% compared to sending from Gmail.
3
Turn on Email Warmup immediately — this is the most important step
In the Email Accounts list, click on the account you just connected. A settings panel opens. Find the Warmup section. Click the toggle to turn it ON. A green dot appears — warmup is now active.
Email warmup means Instantly.ai automatically sends and replies to emails from your account in the background — between real accounts in their warmup network. This builds your sender reputation gradually so email providers see your address as trusted and active.

CRITICAL: Wait 14 days after turning on warmup before sending any cold email campaign. This is non-negotiable. Starting before warmup completes sends your emails straight to spam. Use the 14 days to build your Make.com scenario from Lesson 3 so you are ready to launch the moment warmup completes.

During warmup you will see automated warmup emails in your Sent folder. They have subjects like "Re: Quick check in" — ignore them. They are Instantly.ai's warmup system working correctly.
Step 2 — Create Your Cold Email Campaign
1
Click "Campaigns" in the left sidebar then "+ New Campaign"
In the Instantly.ai left sidebar, click Campaigns. The Campaigns page opens showing all your campaigns (empty for now). Click the + New Campaign button in the top right. A campaign creation wizard opens with numbered steps at the top.
2
Name the campaign and select your sending account
In the campaign name field type something descriptive like Restaurant Owners — [City] — [Month]. This helps you find and compare campaigns later. Below the name, find "Sending Accounts" — click Add account and select the email account you connected in Step 1. Click Continue or Next to proceed.
3
Upload your Apollo CSV — import your prospects
The next step asks you to add leads. Click Upload CSV. A file picker opens. Navigate to your Downloads folder and select the Apollo CSV file you exported in Lesson 1. Click Open. Instantly shows a preview of your CSV with column headers across the top.
Instantly asks you to map columns to variables. Click each dropdown and match them:

— Apollo column "First Name" → map to firstName
— Apollo column "Last Name" → map to lastName
— Apollo column "Company" → map to companyName
— Apollo column "Title" → map to jobTitle
— Apollo column "Email" → map to email (this is auto-mapped)

Click Confirm after mapping all columns. Instantly imports the contacts. You see a count showing how many contacts were successfully imported (skip duplicates if it asks).
4
Write Email 1 — the initial outreach
The next screen is the email editor. Click in the Subject field and type:
Quick question about {{companyName}}
Then click in the email body field and paste this email. Use double curly braces for variables — Instantly replaces them automatically with real data from your CSV:
Initial Outreach Email — Email 1
Hi {{firstName}}, I came across {{companyName}} and noticed you likely receive a lot of customer inquiries — DMs, contact form submissions, emails — that take significant time to respond to each day. I build AI automation systems that reply to every customer message within seconds, 24/7, in your brand voice. Businesses using this system stop losing leads to slow response times. Would a 15-minute call this week make sense to show you exactly how it works for {{companyName}}? [Your Name]
Keep it under 100 words. Short emails outperform long ones in cold outreach. The goal is one thing: get a yes to a 15-minute call. Do not sell on email.
5
Click "+ Add Step" to add a follow-up email
Below the email editor, click + Add Step. A new email editor appears for Email 2. Set the delay to 3 days — Email 2 is sent 3 days after Email 1 if there is no reply. Write the follow-up:
Follow-Up — Email 2 (Day 3)
Hi {{firstName}}, Just following up on my note from a few days ago. If {{companyName}} is losing leads because of slow response times, I can show you a working solution in 15 minutes. Worth a quick look? [Your Name]
Two emails maximum. After two emails with no reply, stop completely. Sending a third or fourth follow-up damages your sender reputation and rarely produces results.
6
Configure sending settings before launching
Click Schedule or Settings in the campaign wizard. Set each option carefully:
Daily sending limit: Set to 20 for week 1, then increase to 50 in week 3. Never start at 50/day — this looks like spam behavior to email providers.

Sending days: Check Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday. Uncheck Saturday and Sunday — cold emails on weekends get ignored.

Sending hours: Set start time to 09:00 and end time to 17:00 in the recipient's timezone. Emails sent during business hours are opened more.

Stop sending when someone replies: Make sure this toggle is ON. This stops follow-ups when someone already replied — critical for looking human.

Open and click tracking: Turn both ON — this lets you see who opened your emails and which emails perform best.
7
Launch the campaign and monitor daily
Click Launch Campaign. Instantly.ai starts sending emails according to your schedule. The campaign dashboard shows real-time stats: emails sent today, total opens, total replies. Check this every morning. When someone replies, their thread appears in the Instantly.ai Unibox — click Unibox in the left sidebar to see all replies.
When you get a positive reply, respond within 1 hour. Suggest two specific times for a call: "Would Tuesday at 2pm or Wednesday at 11am work for you?" Never make them do scheduling work — give them two specific options.
⚠ Do NOT Skip the 14-Day Warmup
This is the single most common mistake beginners make. Sending cold emails before warmup completes sends every email to spam and may get your account suspended. The 14 days feel frustrating but they are the difference between a system that works and one that fails. Use the wait time to build the Make.com scenario in Lesson 3 — you will be ready to launch the moment warmup is done.
Key Takeaways — Lesson 2
Turn on warmup immediately after connecting your email — then wait 14 days before launching any campaign.
Start at 20 emails/day for week 1, then increase to 50/day in week 3. Never start at maximum volume.
Always enable "Stop sending when someone replies" — this prevents follow-ups going to people who already responded.
The goal of every cold email is one specific ask: a 15-minute call. Do not pitch, sell, or explain everything in the email.

Go to instantly.ai and start the free trial. Connect your sending email account. Turn on warmup immediately — the clock starts now. While warmup runs for the next 14 days, build the Lesson 3 Make.com scenario and prepare your campaign content. Set a calendar reminder for 14 days from today labeled "Launch Instantly.ai Campaign." When that reminder fires, your warmup is complete and you can launch.

Step 1 — Set Up the Google Sheet as Prospect Input
1
Create a new Google Sheet and set up column headers
Go to sheets.google.com. Click + Blank to create a new spreadsheet. Click "Untitled spreadsheet" at the top and rename it Outreach Prospects — [Industry]. Click on cell A1 and type the first header. Press Tab to move to B1. Enter all seven headers:
A1: First Name | B1: Last Name | C1: Email | D1: Company | E1: Job Title | F1: Industry | G1: Processed

Column G is critical — Make.com writes "YES" here after processing each row, so it never processes the same person twice.
2
Paste your Apollo CSV data into the sheet
Open your Apollo CSV (the file you downloaded in Lesson 1). Select all the data rows (not the header row). Copy them with Ctrl+C (Windows) or Cmd+C (Mac). Go back to your Google Sheet. Click on cell A2. Paste with Ctrl+V or Cmd+V. The prospect data fills in starting at row 2. Verify the columns match the headers in row 1.
Step 2 — Build the Make.com Automation Scenario
1
Create a new Scenario with Google Sheets "Watch New Rows" trigger
In Make.com, click + Create a new scenario. Click the trigger circle. Search Google Sheets. Click it. Click Watch New Rows. A settings panel opens. Click Add to connect your Google account — sign in and click Allow. Then select your "Outreach Prospects" spreadsheet. Select the correct sheet tab. Set the Limit to 5. Click OK.
Why Limit = 5? If set to 100, Make.com processes all 100 rows simultaneously — this is expensive (100 operations per run) and risks hitting API rate limits. Limit = 5 means each scheduled run processes only 5 prospects at a time, spreading the load over multiple runs and staying within free plan operation limits. Set the scenario schedule to run every 15 minutes.
2
Add OpenAI to generate a unique personalized first line for each prospect
Click + after the Google Sheets module. Add OpenAI → Create a Chat Completion. Connect your OpenAI API key. Set model to gpt-4o. In the Message content field, click Add item, set Role to user, and paste this prompt using variable picker to insert the prospect's data:
Write a single personalized opening sentence for a cold email to {{1.First_Name}} who is a {{1.Job_Title}} at {{1.Company}} in the {{1.Industry}} industry. The sentence should reference something specific about their role or industry that makes it feel personal. It should transition naturally into an email about AI automation services that save business owners time. Write ONLY the one opening sentence. Maximum 25 words. Natural tone — not salesy. No greetings, no sign-off.
Example of what GPT-4o generates: "Managing customer inquiries for a busy restaurant must take an enormous amount of your time every day."

This unique sentence makes every email feel hand-written. Reply rates increase significantly when the first line is personalized versus generic.
3
Find your Instantly.ai API Key
Before building the HTTP module, you need your Instantly API Key. Go to instantly.ai. Click your profile icon in the top right corner. Click API Keys. Click Create API Key. Give it a name like "Make.com Integration." Click Create. A long string appears — copy it immediately and paste it into your notes. You cannot see this key again after closing the popup.
4
Find your Instantly Campaign ID
In Instantly.ai, click Campaigns. Click on the campaign you created in Lesson 2. Look at the URL in your browser's address bar — it looks like https://app.instantly.ai/app/campaign/abc-123-def.... The long string after "/campaign/" is your Campaign ID. Copy it and save it to your notes.
5
Add HTTP module to send the prospect to Instantly via API
Click + after OpenAI. Add HTTP → Make a Request. Fill in the settings:
URL:
https://api.instantly.ai/api/v1/lead/add
Method: POST
Headers — click "Add item":
Name: Content-Type | Value: application/json

Body type: Raw
Content type: JSON (application/json)
Request content (paste this, replace YOUR values):
{ "api_key": "YOUR_INSTANTLY_API_KEY", "campaign_id": "YOUR_CAMPAIGN_ID", "skip_if_in_workspace": true, "leads": [ { "email": "{{1.Email}}", "first_name": "{{1.First_Name}}", "last_name": "{{1.Last_Name}}", "company_name": "{{1.Company}}", "personalization": "{{2.choices[].message.content}}" } ] }
Use variable picker for the email/name/company fields — click the icon and navigate to the Google Sheets module variables. For personalization use the OpenAI module → choices[] → message → content.
6
Mark each processed row with "YES" in column G
After the HTTP module, click +. Add Google Sheets → Update a Row. Select your Outreach Prospects spreadsheet. In the Row Number field use the variable picker and select the Row Number variable from the Google Sheets trigger module. In the Processed column (G), type the value YES directly. Click OK.
This is essential. The "Watch New Rows" trigger only processes rows that have not been seen before. By writing "YES" in column G after processing, you are marking which rows are done. Without this, Make.com would re-process the same prospects every 15 minutes and spam them.
7
Update the Instantly email template to use the personalization variable
Go back to Instantly.ai. Open your campaign. Click Edit on Email 1. In the email body, add the {{personalization}} variable at the very beginning of the email body, on its own line:
{{personalization}} I noticed {{companyName}} likely handles a significant volume of customer inquiries every day that take real time to respond to. I build AI systems that reply to every message within seconds, 24/7, in your brand voice. Would a 15-minute call this week make sense? [Your Name]
The {{personalization}} variable is replaced by the unique GPT-4o sentence generated for each individual prospect. Every email has a unique opening line — but the rest is templated. This balance of personalization and efficiency is what makes the system scalable.
8
Turn on the Make.com scenario and test it
In Make.com, click the Scheduling toggle at the bottom left to turn it ON. Set the schedule to run every 15 minutes. Click Run once right now to test. Watch each module activate one by one — Google Sheets reads a row, OpenAI generates a line, HTTP sends to Instantly. After the run completes, go to Instantly.ai → your campaign → Leads. Verify the test prospect appears there with the personalization text filled in.
