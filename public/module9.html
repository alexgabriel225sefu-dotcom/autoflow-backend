Workflow · Lesson 2

Using AutoFlow in Your Daily Agency Workflow

AutoFlow is not just a tool — it is your daily operating system for running the agency. This lesson shows you exactly how to use it at every stage: prospecting, sales calls, proposal generation, client delivery, and ongoing management.

Before Every Sales Call — Use the Wizard
1
Open AutoFlow 10 minutes before the call
Go to the Automation Wizard. Select the prospect's industry and the automation type you plan to propose. Fill in their pain point if you know it from your research. Click Generate.
2
Study the generated package
Read through what gets built, the Make.com modules, and the recommended price. Now you walk into the call knowing exactly what to propose, how long it takes to build, and what to charge. You sound like an expert who has done this 100 times.
3
Copy the GPT System Message
Copy the generated GPT System Message. After the call, when you build the automation in Make.com, paste it directly into the OpenAI module. This saves 30 minutes of prompt writing per client.
During Prospecting — Use Email Automation
1
Go to Email Automation section
Click the Cold Outreach template. Enter the prospect's name, company, and email. Click Generate with AI + Send. AutoFlow writes a personalized email using their specific details and sends it immediately — all in under 60 seconds.
2
Use the Follow-Up templates
3 days after the initial email with no reply — go back to Email Automation, select the Day 3 Follow-Up template, fill in their details, generate and send. Takes 45 seconds. Do this for every prospect systematically.
After Signing a Client — Use Client Tracker
1
Add them to Client Tracker immediately
Go to Client Tracker → click Add Client. Enter their business name, automation type, and monthly retainer. Set status to Active. Your Dashboard MRR updates instantly.
2
Use AI Assistant for any technical questions
While building their automation, if you hit a problem — open AI Assistant and ask. "How do I parse the WhatsApp message body in Make.com?" "What is the correct HTTP header for 360dialog?" The assistant answers instantly with exact steps.
✓ The 15-Minute Daily Routine
Every morning: open AutoFlow Dashboard — check stats. Send 3 outreach emails using Email Automation — 5 minutes. Check AI Assistant for any client questions — 5 minutes. Update Client Tracker if anything changed — 2 minutes. That is your complete agency management routine. Everything else is automated.
Key Takeaways — Lesson 2

Add every client to the Tracker immediately — your MRR updates automatically.

Sell AutoFlow App Access to Your Clients

Here is an advanced revenue opportunity most people miss: you can sell access to a white-labeled version of AutoFlow to your own clients as an add-on service. They pay you monthly for access to the platform — completely passive income on top of your retainers.

The Concept — White Label the Platform
Your clients — restaurants, agencies, coaches — also need tools to manage their own operations. AutoFlow's AI Assistant, Email templates, and Client Tracker are useful to any business owner. You offer them access as an add-on to their existing service with you.
This works especially well for clients who are also selling services themselves — coaches who want to send outreach emails, consultants who track their own clients, small agencies that need AI tools but can't afford enterprise software.
How to Position and Price It
1
Position as "Your AI Operations Hub"
Do not call it AutoFlow when selling to clients. Call it "[Your Agency Name] Client Portal" or "AI Operations Hub." The pitch: "As part of working with us, you get access to our proprietary AI platform — you can use it to send outreach emails, ask AI questions about your business, and track your own client pipeline."
2
Pricing tiers
Add-on to existing retainer: +$49/month — access to AI Assistant and Email templates only

Standalone access: $97/month — full platform access for clients who want all features

Annual access: $797/year — save 30%, pay once
3
Who to offer it to first
Start with your happiest clients — the ones who already trust you and see results. In your next monthly check-in call, show them the platform live. Let them ask the AI Assistant a question themselves. Most clients say yes immediately when they see it working in real time.

The platform add-on costs you nothing extra — it is already built and running. Every dollar from it is pure profit.
