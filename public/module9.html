PRO Tool · Lesson 1

AutoFlow App — Complete Overview

The AutoFlow app lives at /app on the same domain. It is a dashboard with 7 AI tools designed specifically for automation agency owners. Each tool eliminates a task that would otherwise take 30–60 minutes by hand.

How to Access the App
1
Open your browser and go to the app URL
Navigate to [yourdomain]/app. You will see a login screen with two fields: email and access code. This is the same domain where the course lives — just add /app to the URL. The login page loads in under 2 seconds.
2
Enter your PRO credentials
Type your email address in the first field. Then type your PRO access code in the second field. Your access code was sent to your inbox after purchase — the subject line is "Your AI Cash Systems PRO Access Code." Click Sign In.
If you cannot find your access code: check your spam or promotions folder. If it is still missing, contact support and you will receive a new one within 24 hours.
3
You land on the main dashboard
After clicking Sign In, the dashboard loads. You see a left sidebar with 7 tool tabs. The main area shows whichever tool is currently selected. By default you start on the Blueprint Generator tab. You are now inside the app.
The 7 Tools — What Each One Does
1
AI Blueprint Generator
You describe what you want to automate in plain English. The tool outputs a complete step-by-step Make.com scenario blueprint — every module listed in order, every connection explained, every field specified. Use this before building any new automation for a client. It eliminates the planning phase entirely.
When to use it: Before starting any new Make.com build. During a discovery call when you want to sketch out the plan. When a client describes their use case and you need to figure out how to build it.
2
Make.com Scenario Importer
Paste a blueprint JSON and the tool imports it directly into your Make.com account. Instead of clicking through Make.com's interface to build each module manually, you paste one JSON block and the entire scenario appears, pre-configured. This turns a 3-hour build into a 15-minute one.
When to use it: Any time you have a JSON blueprint — whether from the Blueprint Generator or from a template. Especially useful when replicating the same scenario for a new client.
3
AI Prompt Generator
Generates optimized System prompts for AI bots. You select the bot type (WhatsApp bot, Instagram bot, email reply bot) and describe the business. The tool outputs a full System message ready to paste into your OpenAI module in Make.com. Prompt engineering takes 45 minutes when done manually — this tool does it in 30 seconds.
When to use it: After winning a new client, before building their bot. When a client wants to update the bot's personality or knowledge. When starting a new bot type you haven't built before.
4
Client Proposal Generator
You describe the client's business — industry, size, main problem. The tool generates a ready-to-send proposal document. It includes an executive summary, the proposed automation solution, expected outcomes, pricing, and next steps. The output is formatted professionally and can be pasted into a PDF or sent as a Google Doc.
When to use it: After a discovery call. Before sending a follow-up email. When a prospect asks "can you send me something in writing?" — you can have a proposal ready before they hang up.
5
Cold Email Generator
Enter a target niche (e.g., "dental clinics in London" or "e-commerce stores selling supplements"). The tool writes a 3-email cold outreach sequence: the first email, a Day 3 follow-up, and a Day 7 final follow-up. Each email is personalized to the niche's pain points. All three are ready to copy into your email tool.
When to use it: When starting outreach to a new niche. When your current outreach sequence stops getting replies and you want a fresh angle. Once per niche — the emails work repeatedly.
6
ROI Calculator
Enter the prospect's industry, their estimated number of messages or calls per day, and their staff hourly rate. The calculator shows them exactly how many hours per week are spent on manual responses and converts that to a monthly dollar cost. Then it shows how much an automation costs. The prospect sees their own numbers — not a generic claim.
When to use it: During a sales call while screensharing. Send the screenshot to a hesitant prospect after the call. Embed the numbers in your proposal for context.
7
Monthly Report Generator
Enter last month's stats: messages handled, response time, top topics, uptime. The tool generates a professional client report that looks like it took hours to write. The report includes a summary, key metrics, month-over-month comparison, and a section on what was optimized. Send it to the client via email every month to justify the retainer.
When to use it: On the 1st or 2nd of every month. When a client asks "what did I get this month?" — pull up their stats and generate the report in 3 minutes. It reinforces the value of the service and reduces churn.
Why These 7 Tools Exist
Each tool replaces a task that eats 30–90 minutes when done manually. Using all 7 tools every week saves approximately 8–12 hours of work. That is the difference between running 3 clients and running 10 clients at the same effort level.
Key Takeaways — Lesson 1
The app lives at [yourdomain]/app — log in with your PRO email and access code.
The Blueprint Generator plans your Make.com builds before you open Make.com.
The Proposal Generator creates professional proposals in under 5 minutes during the sales call.
The Monthly Report Generator justifies every retainer with a professional client-facing document.
Workflow · Lesson 2

Using AutoFlow in Your Daily Agency Workflow

This is a complete day-in-the-life walkthrough of an automation agency owner using the app. Every step is explained click by click — morning routine, sales prep, client delivery, and monthly reporting.

Morning Routine — 15 Minutes
1
Open the app at [yourdomain]/app
Every morning, open the app as the first thing you do. Log in if your session has expired. You land on the dashboard. Scan the left sidebar — notice which tools you will need today based on what is on your schedule.
2
Check any pending tasks or client notes
Review your client list mentally. Do you have a sales call today? Do any existing clients need a monthly report? Are you starting a new automation build? Each situation calls for different tools. Planning which tools you will use takes 2 minutes and sets direction for the day.
3
Use the Blueprint Generator for any new automations needed
Click Blueprint Generator in the left sidebar. In the text field that appears, describe the automation you need to plan today. For example: "WhatsApp bot for a dental clinic that handles appointment booking, opening hours questions, and directions." Click Generate Blueprint. Read the output — this is your build plan for the day.
Before a Sales Call — 10 Minutes of Prep
4
Use the ROI Calculator — enter the prospect's numbers
Click ROI Calculator in the sidebar. Fill in: the prospect's industry (e.g., "dental clinic"), their estimated messages or calls per day (estimate from research — dental clinics typically get 40–80 calls/day), and their staff hourly rate (use industry average if unknown — $20/hour is safe for most). Click Calculate.
The calculator shows: hours per week spent on manual replies, monthly cost of that time, automation setup cost, automation monthly cost, and break-even timeline. Screenshot this result. You will share your screen showing these numbers during the call.
5
Use the Proposal Generator — ready before the call ends
Click Proposal Generator. In the description field, type: the client's business name, their industry, their main problem, and what you plan to build for them. Click Generate Proposal. The proposal appears — read it once to confirm it makes sense for this client. Copy it into a Google Doc. By the time the call ends, you can send them the link.
The proposal takes 4–5 minutes to generate and review. This means you can literally say during the call: "I'll send you a written proposal in the next 10 minutes." And you can. Prospects love this responsiveness — it signals professionalism.
After Signing a New Client
6
Use the Blueprint Generator for their specific use case
Click Blueprint Generator. This time, be specific: include the client's actual business name, their exact use case, specific integrations they need (e.g., Google Calendar for bookings, their CRM name). Click Generate Blueprint. The output is now a detailed build plan customized for this client. Save it somewhere accessible.
7
Use the Prompt Generator — create the bot's System message
Click Prompt Generator. Select the bot type from the dropdown — for most clients this is WhatsApp Bot. In the business description field, write everything the bot needs to know: services offered, prices, opening hours, location, booking process, FAQs. Click Generate Prompt. Copy the output — this is your OpenAI System message.
Paste the generated System message directly into the OpenAI module in your Make.com scenario. This is the instruction set the AI follows when replying to the client's customers. The more detail you put in the description, the better the bot performs.
8
Build in Make.com using the blueprint as a checklist
Open Make.com in a separate tab. Keep the blueprint visible in the AutoFlow app. Work through the blueprint module by module — it tells you exactly which modules to add, in what order, and how to configure each one. Treat it as a checklist: check off each module as you add it to the scenario.
Blueprint Generator — Step-by-Step Walkthrough
1
Click "Blueprint Generator" in the left sidebar
The Blueprint Generator panel opens in the main content area. You see a large text field with a placeholder that says "Describe the automation you want to build..." Below it is a blue Generate Blueprint button.
2
Describe the automation in detail
Click inside the text field. Type your description. Example:
WhatsApp bot for a dental clinic that handles appointment booking, sends appointment reminders 24 hours before the appointment, answers questions about services and pricing, and escalates to a human when the customer requests it.
The more specific your description, the better the blueprint. Include: what triggers the automation, what data needs to be captured, what happens with that data, and what the output or response is.
3
Click "Generate Blueprint" and wait 10–20 seconds
Click the Generate Blueprint button. A loading indicator appears. After 10–20 seconds, the blueprint appears below. It is structured as a numbered list of Make.com modules with explanations of what each one does and how to configure it.
4
Read through the generated plan carefully
The blueprint explains every module needed in Make.com: the Webhook trigger, the OpenAI module, the HTTP module for sending the WhatsApp reply, the Google Calendar module for booking, the date formatter for reminders, and the filter for escalation. Each module comes with the exact settings to use.
5
Use it as a checklist while building
Open Make.com in a separate browser tab. Keep the blueprint visible. Add each module to your Make.com scenario in the order listed. Refer back to the blueprint for field values, connection types, and filter conditions. When every item is checked, your scenario is complete.
Monthly Reporting — Once Per Month Per Client
9
Use the Monthly Report Generator
Click Monthly Report Generator in the sidebar. Fill in the stats fields: total messages handled this month, average response time in seconds, most common topics (enter 3–5), percentage of messages handled automatically vs escalated, and any notable events this month. Click Generate Report.
The report takes 15–30 seconds to generate. When it appears, review it once. Copy it into an email or a PDF. Send it to the client with a short note: "Here is your monthly automation report for [month]. The bot handled X messages, saving approximately Y hours of staff time. Let me know if you have any questions." This email takes 3 minutes total.
The 15-Minute Daily Agency Routine
Morning: Open app, generate blueprint for any new build (5 min). Outreach: Use Cold Email Generator for new niches (3 min per niche, once per week). Sales prep: ROI Calculator + Proposal Generator before every call (10 min). Post-signature: Blueprint Generator + Prompt Generator (15 min). Monthly: Report Generator for every active client (5 min per client). Everything else is building in Make.com — and the blueprint tells you exactly what to build.
Exercise — Run the Full Workflow Once
Pick a fictional dental clinic client. Open the app and do all of this: (1) Use the ROI Calculator with 60 messages/day at $22/hour. Screenshot the result. (2) Use the Proposal Generator — write 3 sentences about this fake clinic, generate the proposal. (3) Use the Blueprint Generator — describe a WhatsApp bot for them. (4) Use the Prompt Generator to get the System message. By the end you will have completed the full client workflow and know every tool by touch.
Key Takeaways — Lesson 2
Use the ROI Calculator and Proposal Generator before every sales call — arrive prepared with numbers and a document.
After signing a client: Blueprint Generator first, then Prompt Generator, then build in Make.com.
The Monthly Report Generator handles all client reporting in 5 minutes per client — do it on the 1st of every month.
All 7 tools together replace approximately 8–12 hours of manual work per week.
Revenue · Lesson 3

Sell AutoFlow App Access to Your Clients

App access can be an upsell — give clients access to specific tools, charge a monthly fee, and earn passive income on top of your retainers. This lesson shows you exactly how to position it, price it, and close the upsell.

The ROI Calculator as a Closing Tool
1
Walk through the ROI Calculator on a screenshare during the sales call
During the call, say: "Let me show you something." Share your screen. Open the AutoFlow app and navigate to ROI Calculator. Ask the prospect: "How many WhatsApp messages or phone calls do you get per day on average?" Enter their answer. Ask: "And roughly how much do you pay staff per hour?" Enter that too. Click Calculate.
The calculator appears in real time on their screen. You are entering their own numbers — not fabricated statistics. This is the critical psychological difference: they see their own situation reflected back at them.
2
Read the result out loud with them
Read the output together. For example: "You get 50 WhatsApp messages per day. You spend 3 minutes per reply. That is 2.5 hours per day — 50 hours per month. At $20 per hour, that is $1,000 per month in staff time just answering WhatsApp. Our automation handles all of that for $300 per month."
The silence after you read those numbers is where the deal closes. The prospect does the math themselves. They are not being sold — they are looking at their own data and drawing the conclusion. Your job at this point is to not speak. Let the numbers breathe.
3
The psychological impact of their own numbers
A salesperson saying "automation saves businesses thousands per month" triggers skepticism. The same number appearing from a calculator they just filled in with their own data triggers belief. This is the core reason the ROI Calculator converts. Never skip it on a sales call.
Giving Clients Access — The Upsell Structure
4
How to create a client login
The app uses access codes. To give a client their own access: go to your admin settings within the app, navigate to Access Codes, and generate a new code. Assign it to the client's email address. The client then logs in at [yourdomain]/app using their email and the code you created. They see only the tools you have enabled for their account.
What clients can do with their access: view the ROI Calculator, generate their own monthly summary reports, browse proposal templates. What they cannot do: access your blueprint builds, see other clients' data, or use admin functions.
5
Upsell the "Premium Client Dashboard" for $50/month
After a client has been with you for 2–3 months and is happy, pitch them access to the Monthly Report Generator. Frame it as: "Right now I generate your monthly reports. For $50 extra per month, I can give you direct access to our client portal — you can run your own reports any time, see your automation stats, and track your ROI in real time."
Most clients say yes immediately. They like feeling in control of the data. They like being able to show their own boss or stakeholders the results without waiting for you. The $50/month costs you nothing additional — it is pure margin.
Using the Proposal Generator for Every New Pitch
6
Generate a professional proposal in 5 minutes
After every discovery call, before sending a follow-up email: open the app, click Proposal Generator, fill in the client's industry and main use case, click Generate Proposal. The output is a PDF-quality proposal with sections: Executive Summary, Proposed Solution, Expected Outcomes, Investment, and Next Steps.
The proposal takes approximately 20–30 seconds to generate. Then spend 3–4 minutes personalizing: add the prospect's name to the header, insert their actual number from the ROI Calculator, and adjust the pricing line to match your quote from the call. Save as a PDF and attach to your follow-up email.
7
What to write when sending the proposal
Send the follow-up email within 1 hour of the call. Subject line: "Proposal for [Business Name] — WhatsApp Automation". Email body:
Hi [Name], Great talking with you today. As promised, here is the proposal we discussed. Based on the numbers we looked at together, your current manual reply workload costs approximately $[X]/month in staff time. The automation covers itself in the first [X] weeks. The proposal PDF is attached. It outlines exactly what gets built, the timeline, and the investment. To move forward, simply reply to this email and I will send the invoice. We can have your system live within [X] days. Let me know if you have any questions. [Your name]
Send it within the hour. Prospects make decisions when the energy of the call is still fresh. A proposal sent 3 days later competes with the prospect's changing priorities, other vendors, and forgotten context. Speed is the differentiator.
Common Mistake to Avoid
Do not give all 7 tools to clients on their first upsell. Start with just the ROI Calculator and Monthly Report Generator — these are the two tools clients immediately understand. If you open up the Blueprint Generator and Prompt Generator, clients may attempt to build things themselves and undervalue your service. Restrict access to tools that serve the client relationship without replacing your billable work.
Exercise — Prepare Your Upsell Pitch
Write down the names of your 3 most satisfied current clients. For each one: (1) use the ROI Calculator with their actual numbers to remind yourself of the value you deliver, (2) use the Monthly Report Generator with last month's stats to create a report for them right now, (3) draft a one-paragraph pitch for the $50/month portal access. Practice delivering that pitch out loud before your next client check-in call.
Key Takeaways — Lesson 3
The ROI Calculator closes deals — always screenshare it during calls and enter the prospect's real numbers.
Give clients access to the Report Generator as a $50/month "Client Portal" upsell — pure margin, zero extra work.
Send the proposal within 1 hour of the call — speed signals professionalism and captures momentum.
Restrict client access to tools that serve the relationship — do not hand over blueprint-building tools.
