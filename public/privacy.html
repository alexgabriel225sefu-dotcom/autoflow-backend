2. What Data We Collect

We collect only the minimum data necessary to provide our services:

2a. Trading Bot Products (Apex Trade Bot / Apex Forex Bot)

If you purchase our trading bot software, the following applies in addition:

  • Hosted trading service: the bots are fully hosted — we run them 24/7 on our servers. To trade on your behalf you connect your own cTrader account (any cTrader-compatible broker) via secure authentication. This connection is used solely to place the trades you configure. We never request withdrawal permissions and never have access to your funds; you can revoke access at your broker at any time.
  • Trading data we process: to operate your bot we process your account balance, open positions, and trade history. This is used only to run and display your bot — it is never sold or shared for marketing.
  • Third-party services the bot uses on your behalf: your connected cTrader broker, AI providers (Anthropic, with Groq as fallback) for signal generation, and Telegram for notifications. These connections are governed by those providers' privacy policies in addition to ours.
  • What we store about bot customers: your purchase email, your license key, payment records (via Digistore24), your encrypted cTrader connection credentials, and your bot configuration (pairs, risk settings).

6. Cookies and Local Storage

We use only strictly necessary cookies and browser storage. We do not use advertising, tracking, or profiling cookies.

  • Digistore24 payment cookies: Set by Digistore24 when you interact with our checkout. These are strictly necessary to process payments securely and for fraud-prevention. Digistore24's privacy policy: digistore24.com/privacy-policy.
  • Access cookie (af_access): An HttpOnly, signed cookie that verifies your course purchase — required for access to course content.
  • Consent cookie (ck_consent): Stored in your browser's localStorage to remember whether you have acknowledged this cookie notice. Not a tracking cookie; never transmitted to any server.
  • Session storage: Short-lived browser-side data (e.g. affiliate referral code) used to complete the current checkout. Not a cookie; cleared when your browser tab closes.

Page fonts are loaded from Google Fonts and Fontshare (fonts.googleapis.com, api.fontshare.com), which may process your IP address as part of the font request; no personal data beyond the IP is shared with these services for advertising purposes.

Because we use only strictly necessary cookies (payment processing), no cookie consent is required under EU ePrivacy rules. The consent banner on our pages is informational — you can dismiss it at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at supportaicashsystem@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority.
