If you purchase our trading bot software, the following applies in addition:

7. Cookies and Local Storage

We use only strictly necessary cookies and browser storage. We do not use advertising, tracking, or profiling cookies.

  • Digistore24 payment cookies: set when you interact with our checkout, strictly necessary for secure payment processing and fraud prevention.
  • Access cookie (af_access): an HttpOnly, signed cookie that verifies your purchase — required for access to purchased content.
  • Consent cookie (ck_consent): stored in your browser's localStorage to remember whether you have acknowledged this cookie notice. Not a tracking cookie; never transmitted to any server.
  • Session storage: short-lived browser-side data (e.g. affiliate referral code) used to complete the current checkout. Cleared when your browser tab closes.

Because we use only strictly necessary cookies, no cookie consent is required under EU ePrivacy rules — the notice on our pages is informational and can be dismissed at any time.
