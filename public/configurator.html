Choose Your Trading Coin
Select the cryptocurrency your bot will trade. You can change this later.
Choose Your Risk Level
This controls how much of your balance is risked per trade.
Choose Your Strategies
All three strategies work together. Disable any you don't want active.
Choose Your Timeframe
Controls how frequently your bot checks for trade signals.
5m = more trades, more risk  |  15m = balanced (recommended)  |  1h = fewer, calmer trades  |  4h = swing trading, very selective
