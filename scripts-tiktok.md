## Script 1 — "I Was There Too" (Empathy hook)

**[0–3s] HOOK**
"I know what it feels like to work a job you hate
and still not have enough money at the end of the month."
*(Quiet voice, direct look — not performing, just real)*

**[3–20s] EMPATHY**
"You do everything right. You show up. You work hard.
You try to save.
And somehow it's still not enough.
And the worst part? You don't even know what to change.
Because nobody taught you there was another way."

**[20–45s] TURNING POINT**
"A year ago I was exactly there.
I started looking for something — anything — that didn't
require a degree, a huge investment, or years of grinding.
I found AI automation. I was skeptical.
I tried it anyway.
Within a month I had my first client.
Not because I'm special. Because the skill is real."

**[45–60s] CLOSE**
"I'm not here to sell you a dream.
I just want to show you what's possible
when you decide to learn one thing properly.
Follow along — I'll show you everything."

📍 **Caption:** If you're tired of working hard and still feeling behind — this is for you #mindset #motivation #lifechange #AIautomation

## Filming Guide

| Element | How to do it |
|---------|-------------|
| **Camera** | Phone front camera, held at eye level or slightly above |
| **Lighting** | Natural light from a window facing you |
| **Background** | Desk, couch, bedroom — casual and real |
| **Outfit** | Hoodie or t-shirt — authentic beats polished |
| **Delivery** | Slow down on emotional lines. Pause after hooks. |
| **Captions** | Add with CapCut (auto-caption) — always on |
| **Music** | None or very low background — let the words hit |
