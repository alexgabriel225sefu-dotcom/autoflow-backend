Your funds are safe.

We designed every layer of the system around one principle: you stay in control.

Trade-only API keys

The bot connects to your broker with restricted API permissions. Only trading is allowed — withdrawals, transfers, and account changes are blocked at the broker level.

Your account, your control

Your funds never leave your cTrader account. We never have access to your balance. You can revoke the API key instantly from your broker settings.

Automatic risk management

Every trade has a stop-loss set automatically. Max drawdown caps and daily loss limits prevent catastrophic losses. A flash-crash guard pauses trading during extreme volatility.

Full transparency

Every trade entry and exit is reported to you via Telegram in real-time, including the AI's reasoning. Use /status and /report to check your balance and P&L at any time.
