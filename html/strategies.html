Three strategies. One bot.

Switch between AI signals, grid trading, and DCA accumulation with a single Telegram command.

AI Signal Trading

The bot uses machine-learning signals to detect high-probability entries. It waits for confluence across multiple indicators before placing a trade.

Best for: Trending markets, hands-off trading

Grid Trading

Places buy and sell orders at fixed intervals around the current price. Profits from every oscillation in ranging markets — automatically.

Best for: Sideways markets, consistent small gains

DCA (Dollar Cost Average)

Buys a fixed amount at regular intervals regardless of price. Ideal for long-term accumulation with lower average entry.

Best for: Long-term holding, accumulation phases
